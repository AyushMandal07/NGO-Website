Our Aim

At Prosostho, we're dedicated to uplifting children and impoverished communities in West Bengal by providing essential support, education, and opportunities. Through our efforts, we aim to empower individuals to break the cycle of poverty and build a brighter future.

Messege on Whatsapp

What we do

Educate Children

In our education initiatives, we prioritize nurturing young minds, providing access to quality learning resources, and fostering a supportive environment where children can thrive academically.

Build Infrastructure

We focus on constructing essential facilities such as schools, community centers, and healthcare facilities to improve the living conditions and access to services for marginalized communities.

Health and Nutrition

We prioritize the well-being of children and marginalized communities in West Bengal. Through comprehensive healthcare services, including medical camps and access to essentials.

Livelihood Development

We empower individuals through skill-building and entrepreneurship support, fostering economic independence through vocational training and microfinance opportunities.
